Nairobi Commuter & County Service Notice

Official Public Board

1. Matatu Stage Operations & Transport Updates

Leo tuko rada ya mathree kwa stage ya mtaani, mbogi mzima iko form ya sherehe kuelekea CBD.

Wasee wote wameshika luku fiti, wanadhokoma kuelekea CBD bila noma yoyote ya usalama.

2. Small Business & County Licensing Guidance

Buda usipanic, chapaa imelipwa na morio wa gava tayari bila ngori.

  • Finje na ashu zimekubaliwa kwa huduma zote za kaunti.
  • Hakuna karao au sanse atakayesumbua biashara zilizosajiliwa.
  • Niaje maze, keja iko poa lakini mullah imekatika kuruka wiki hii.

3. Youth Innovation & Creative Economy

Bazenga na bazuu wa viwanda wameleta ganji ya kusaidia vijana mtaani ili waache ngori na waanzishe biashara.
